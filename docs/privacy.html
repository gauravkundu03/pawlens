PawLens ("the app") is a free student project that classifies photos as cat or dog. This policy explains what data the app handles. The app is available at https://pawlens-gk-dlp.streamlit.app.

1. Information we collect

  • Google account information (only if you sign in): your name and email address, received through Google Sign-In. We do not receive your Google password and do not access your contacts, Drive, Gmail or any other Google data.
  • Sign-in activity: the date and time of your first and latest sign-in, and how many times you signed in.
  • Prediction history (only if you are signed in): for each analysis, the file name, the result (Cat, Dog or Uncertain), the probabilities, the confidence score and the time.

2. Photos you upload

Your photos are processed in memory to produce a prediction. The app does not store your photos. Only the text details listed above are saved for signed-in users. Guests who do not sign in have nothing saved except temporary data in their own browser session.

3. How we use the information

  • To sign you in and show your saved prediction history.
  • To let the app owner see how many people use the app and keep it working.

We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising and do not share it with third parties except the service providers below.

4. Service providers

  • Google (Sign-In / OAuth) for authentication.
  • Streamlit Community Cloud to host the app.
  • MongoDB Atlas to store account and history records.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

5. Retention and your choices

  • Signed-in users can delete their saved prediction history at any time from the History tab.
  • To have your account record (name, email, sign-in details) deleted, email the contact address below and we will remove it.
  • You can use the app as a guest without signing in.

6. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored in a password-protected database. No online service is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
