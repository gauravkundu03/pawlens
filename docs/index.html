A closer look at your furry friends. PawLens is a free web app that tells you whether a photo shows a cat or a dog. It uses a fine-tuned MobileNetV2 deep-learning model that reached 98.4% accuracy on a held-out test set.

Open the app

What you can do

  • Upload a JPG, PNG, BMP or WebP photo and get Cat or Dog with probabilities and a confidence score.
  • Use the app as a guest, or sign in with Google to keep your prediction history between visits.
  • See how the model compares with other approaches on the Model insights tab.

Why Google sign-in?

Signing in with Google lets PawLens recognise you so your saved history is available next time. PawLens only receives your name and email address from Google. See the Privacy Policy for details.
